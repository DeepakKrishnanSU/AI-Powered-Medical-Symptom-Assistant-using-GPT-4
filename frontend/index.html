Critical Medical Red Flags

                        
Signs indicating immediate emergency medical evaluation is needed

                    

                

                
            

            
            

                
If you or someone around you experiences any of the following symptoms, stop using this application and call emergency services (e.g. 911 / 112 / 999) or proceed to the nearest emergency department immediately:

                

                    

                        
Chest & Circulatory

                        
    
                            
  • Crushing, squeezing, or radiating chest pain
    • 
                            
  • Chest pressure spreading to arm, jaw, neck, or back
    • 
                            
  • Sudden irregular heart rate or collapse
    • 
                        

                    

                    

                        
Respiratory

                        
    
                            
  • Severe difficulty breathing or suffocating feeling
    • 
                            
  • Bluish discoloration of lips, face, or fingernails
    • 
                            
  • Inability to speak in full sentences due to air hunger
    • 
                        

                    

                    

                        
Neurological

                        
    
                            
  • Sudden weakness or numbness on one side of body
    • 
                            
  • Facial drooping or slurred / incoherent speech
    • 
                            
  • Sudden severe headache ("worst headache of life")
    • 
                            
  • Loss of consciousness, fainting, or sudden confusion
    • 
                        

                    

                    

                        
Systemic & Allergic

                        
    
                            
  • Signs of anaphylaxis (throat swelling, tongue swelling)
    • 
                            
  • High persistent fever (>103°F / 39.5°C) with stiff neck
    • 
                            
  • Coughing up blood or vomiting blood / coffee-ground emesis
    •
